Course Overview

The Graphics Design course introduces students to the principles, tools, and techniques used to create professional visual content for brands, businesses, and digital media.

You’ll learn how to design visually appealing materials — from social media graphics and marketing content to brand identities and print designs — using industry-standard tools like Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Canva, and Figma.

This beginner-friendly course focuses on both creativity and strategy, helping you turn design concepts into impactful visuals that communicate clearly and attract audiences.

Learning Outcome

    • Understand core design principles including color theory, typography, balance, and composition.
    • Use design tools like Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and Canva to create professional designs for web and print.
    • Design marketing and brand assets that align with client or business objectives.
    • Build a professional portfolio and confidently take on freelance or agency design projects.
    •

What You Will Learn

Go from Idea to Impact, Graphics Designer in 3-6 Months.

Module 1 - Introduction, Course Overview & Design Tools
  1. Introduction to Photoshop, Canva, and Illustrator
  2. Key digital skills
Module 2 - Learning to See Like a Designer
  1. Observation as a core design skill
  2. Visual literacy: understanding positive/negative space, focal points, and relationships
  3. The basics of Gestalt—seeing unified wholes, not just parts
Module 3 - Contrast & Composition
  1. Understanding contrast and its impact on design
  2. Working with figure/ground, hierarchy, and emphasis
  3. Creating rhythm and movement through composition
Module 4 - Introduction to Type & Hierarchy
  1. Basics of typography in visual design
  2. Using contrast in type (size, weight, color) to create hierarchy
  3. Combining type with other visual elements for clarity
Module 5 - Color Theory & Visual Impact
  1. Introduction to color theory and color psychology
  2. Creating emphasis and mood with color and value
  3. Using color to clarify and organize information
Module 6 - Grids, Guides, and Creating Movement
  1. Using grids, columns, and ratios to organize design
  2. Creating movement and flow in still images
  3. Using alignment and rhythm for clarity and energy
Module 7 - The Life of a Designer
  1. Guest interview with a professional designer (industry insights, Q&A)
  2. Discussion of real-world process, challenges, and opportunities
Module 8 - Studio Practice & Class Q&A
  1. Group review of common challenges and design questions
  2. Recap of key skills from previous modules
  3. Open Q&A, troubleshooting, and peer support
Module 9 - Advanced Typography & Simplifying Design
  1. Using typography and shape language to clarify messages
  2. Making complex ideas simple through effective visual choices
  3. Case studies and examples
Module 10 - Message & Meaning in Design
  1. Combining type, color, shape, and layout for strong communication
  2. Simplification strategies: clarity without losing depth
  3. Assignment
Module 11 - Client Project (The Final Boss)
  1. Project briefing: respond to a real or simulated client need
  2. Steps: interpret brief, plan, execute, and revise based on feedback
  3. Assignment:
Module 12 - Portfolio & Next Steps
  1. Selecting work for your portfolio and writing project descriptions
  2. Best practices for presenting digital portfolios (PDF, web, Behance)
  3. Preparing for job search or freelance opportunities
